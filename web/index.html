Welcome

Access Policy Notice

Student laboratory machines (VLAN 70) are not permitted to reach the Administration network (192.168.10.0/24) or the Faculty network (192.168.60.0/24). They retain full access to DHCP, DNS and this web portal. Attempts to reach restricted networks are dropped by ACL 110 on the core router.
